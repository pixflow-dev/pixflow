Know the limits

  • Testnet only. This console talks to Robinhood Chain testnet (46630). Mainnet is refused until explicitly enabled — even if your wallet is on mainnet, the console will not send anything there.
  • The registry is a trust point. The keeper decides which token is canonical. A compromised keeper key can re-point a symbol after a 1h public timelock. The timelock buys time to react; it is not a veto.
  • "Real" is not "good". The router answers "is this the real DCTY?", not "is DCTY a good buy?". The canonical token can still dump after graduation.
  • Capped buys still pay the full fee. Send more than the remaining sellable allocation and the router buys the whole allocation and refunds the rest — but the 0.3% fee is charged on the full amount sent.
